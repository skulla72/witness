# Store-billing classification — short owner/legal review

**UNFINISHED INTERNAL DRAFT — NATIVE BILLING NOT VALIDATED; NO COMPLIANCE CLAIM**

Before choosing billing, record a decision for each actual SKU/service and target
storefront. Do not require identical billing for everything called “counseling.”

- **Classify the actual service:** live 1:1 counseling between two individuals;
  group digital access/one-to-few or one-to-many sessions; replay/recorded content;
  and regulated clinical services by/on behalf of licensed healthcare providers
  diagnosing or managing medical conditions. Verify the actual provider, purpose,
  audience and replay availability; a service label alone is insufficient.
- **Apple:** App Review Guidelines **3.1.3(d)** permit non-IAP methods for real-time
  person-to-person services between two individuals (including medical
  consultations); one-to-few/one-to-many real-time services require IAP under that
  rule. Review digital group access under **3.1.1** and applicable exceptions.
  Separately assess storefront-specific external-link/entitlement rules; do not
  assume that a web checkout or the 1:1 exception covers groups.
- **Google:** Payments policy generally requires Play billing for in-app digital
  access unless an exclusion/program applies. Its FAQ's **1:1 online paid service**
  exception requires two individuals and no recording/replay available in a
  Play-distributed app. The **regulated clinical services** FAQ is a distinct
  exclusion: those transactions **should not use Play billing**, where the
  licensed-provider/medical-condition criteria are actually met. Do not infer
  either clinical status or Apple-equivalent treatment for all counseling/groups.
- **Fundraising:** verify each actual beneficiary's nonprofit/tax-exempt status,
  authority, funds flow and receipt/deductibility claims. Common Light LLC's
  Colorado registration proves none of these. Apple **3.2.1(vi)** addresses
  approved nonprofits, Apple Pay and approval of listed nonprofit beneficiaries;
  Google's tax-exempt-donation exclusion is not an exemption for every “gift.”
- **Choose markets/programs:** record Apple storefronts and Google countries,
  transaction category, any external-link/alternative-billing program,
  eligibility/enrollment/entitlements, disclosures, fees/reporting and reviewer
  evidence. Check current region-specific terms before launch. No program has
  been selected, enabled or validated by this document; Stripe integration or
  embedded web checkout is not native-store billing approval.
