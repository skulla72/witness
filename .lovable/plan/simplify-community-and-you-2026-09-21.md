# Simplify Community and You

## Goal
Make the app easier to understand before the directories fill up. Community will own people, circles, churches, nonprofits, and ways to help; You will show only the member’s own activity and account.

## Changes

### Community tab
- Rename the bottom tab and page from **Walk With** to **Community**.
- Turn the top of the page into three clear choices:
  1. **My circles** — circles already joined.
  2. **Find a circle** — faith-based and open circles, using consistent labels.
  3. **Churches & organizations** — search churches, nonprofits, ministries, and their groups or events.
- Put the organization directory directly into this flow instead of sending people through a promotional card.
- Keep urgent help visible, but reduce it to a compact safety link so it does not overpower the normal page.
- Show only a short preview of available circles on the first screen, with a clear way to see all, preventing a long wall of cards.

### You tab
- Keep the photo, name, and personal prayer summary.
- Replace overlapping shortcuts with four clear destinations: **Messages**, **My prayers**, **My people**, and **Giving**.
- Rename the private “Circle” shortcut to **My people** so it cannot be confused with Community circles.
- Move the member’s prayer cards into a compact preview with a link to the full journal.
- Replace the current catch-all menu with three concise groups:
  - **Your activity:** gratitude journal, giving history, gifts, hours served.
  - **Your organizations:** church, nonprofit, or professional pages the member manages.
  - **Settings:** profile, experience choices, notifications, orders, moderation, sign out.
- Remove repeated links, especially Gifts and Community.

### Giving page cleanup
- Reorder the page around one main action: **Choose where to give**.
- Put nonprofit search and the weekly spotlight first; populated organizations will strengthen these areas without changing the layout again.
- Group the needs network, split fund, and Witness support beneath a quieter “More ways to give” section.
- Move team-only tools out of the public giving flow into the existing admin area.

## Technical notes
- Preserve all existing pages, permissions, data, and faith/non-faith visibility rules.
- Reuse the current visual system and controls; this is an information-order and wording change, not a redesign.
- Keep the five-tab structure and central Share button.
- Verify the revised screens on phone and desktop, including empty and populated states.
