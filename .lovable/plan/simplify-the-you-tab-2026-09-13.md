# Simplify the You tab

## What will change
- Keep the profile photo, name, and three personal stats at the top.
- Replace the long list with four high-value shortcuts: Messages, Journal, Prayer Circle, and Gifts.
- Add one clear **Menu** row that opens a focused full-screen panel, similar to Instagram or Facebook settings.
- Organize the remaining links inside that panel into compact groups: Faith, Community & Service, Giving, and Account.
- Move profile editing, order history, moderation, sign out, and account controls into the panel so the main You tab stays calm.
- Keep the user’s prayers visible below the shortcuts.

## Interaction details
- The panel slides in from the right and has a persistent back button.
- Selecting a link closes the panel and opens that page.
- Account controls expand only when requested, rather than filling the main page.
- The menu remains comfortable on phones and keyboard/screen-reader accessible.

## Verification
- Check signed-in and signed-out states.
- Test opening, closing, and navigating from the menu on a phone-sized screen.
- Confirm profile photo editing, sign out, moderation access, and account controls still work.
