# Neutral survey language

## Goal
Make the survey respect the language choice made during welcome. The plain version will contain no references to God, faith, prayer, scripture, church, or gender-specific experiences. Survey examples will be gender neutral.

## Changes
- Build the survey from the saved language preference instead of showing one universal question set.
- In the plain version, remove the faith-specific question and the faith-specific closed-room section.
- Replace gendered examples such as “one man” and “men’s suicide rates” with inclusive language.
- Use neutral names and descriptions for suggested spaces in the plain results screen, and prevent faith-only spaces from appearing there.
- Keep the faith-based version’s religious wording where it is relevant, while using gender-neutral wording throughout the shared questions.
- Recalculate the question count and progress bar from the version actually being shown.

## Verification
- Open the setup flow after choosing the plain option and confirm no prohibited religious or gendered wording appears anywhere in the survey or results.
- Open the faith-based path and confirm its intended religious questions still appear.
- Confirm progress, saved answers, and completion continue to work on both paths.
