# Simplify Community around Pray, Help, and Give

## Goal
Make Community feel calm and immediately understandable. The first screen will ask what the person wants to do, then lead them into one clear path without removing existing circles, organizations, needs, or giving options.

## Community screen
- Replace the current circles-first opening with three prominent choices: **Pray**, **Help**, and **Give**.
- **Pray** leads to community prayer and supportive circles.
- **Help** leads to practical needs where someone can offer time or skills.
- **Give** leads to a new simple choice screen.
- Keep “My circles” visible below the three choices for returning members.
- Move discovery into a quieter “Explore community” area containing circles and organizations, so it no longer competes with the main actions.
- Keep urgent help information at the bottom.

## Simple Give screen
- Simplify the existing Giving opening into three direct choices:
  1. **Fund a specific need** — concrete work with progress and outcomes.
  2. **Give to an organization** — search churches and nonprofits.
  3. **Support Witness** — direct support for the platform.
- Keep gift history, saved payment methods, thank-you status, and additional giving options below the main choices rather than before them.
- Preserve the existing real checkout and payment destinations; this is a clarity change, not a change to money handling.

## Coordination details
- Use the same Pray / Help / Give language wherever Community points into these areas.
- Keep phone behavior compact while using the wider tablet and desktop space already available.
- Preserve the faith/plain-language preference and existing privacy and safety behavior.

## Verification
- Check the Community and Give paths on phone, tablet, and desktop.
- Confirm all new choices open the intended working screens with no overflow or preview errors.
- Sync the iPhone and Android versions after the changes.
